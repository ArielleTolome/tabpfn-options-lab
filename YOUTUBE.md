## Description

A reproducible options-research demonstration by Ariel Tolome. TabPFN Options Lab compares genuine TabPFN-3.5 inference with chronologically tuned XGBoost and a historical target-success-rate baseline, using the same eligible training history.

Repository and run instructions:
https://github.com/ArielleTolome/tabpfn-options-lab

Research paper:
https://github.com/ArielleTolome/tabpfn-options-lab/tree/main/research

The key rule: a training outcome must resolve strictly before the prediction timestamp. The report exposes probabilities, Brier scores, training counts, and label cutoffs.

IMPORTANT: the runnable demonstration uses synthetic inputs. Its scores are not evidence of trading profitability, model superiority on real markets, or probability calibration. The original research paper concerns TabPFN-3; its historical comparative ML experiment remains unavailable pending authorized data export. We do not relabel V3 results as V3.5. No live orders are sent.

This is an academic software demonstration, not investment advice. Options carry substantial risk. AI tools assisted with implementation, writing, and animation. Narration is AI-generated using Ariel's authorized ElevenLabs voice clone; no institutional endorsement is implied.
