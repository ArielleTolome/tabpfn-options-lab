Why the chronology matters

Filter on label_time < entry_time first, then keep the latest 5,000 eligible entries. Resolved earlier candidates can join later contexts. XGBoost tunes only within prior data: the last 20% validate, and training labels that overlap their first entry are purged.

What this does not measure

No option price paths, executable quotes, fees, spread costs, portfolio returns, Sharpe ratio, or drawdown are modeled. The empirical rate is a probability baseline, not a reconstructed mechanical trading strategy. Tiers are not fractional Kelly sizing.

Model identity and first-cutoff provenance

Loaded identity is checked at runtime. The JSON includes every cutoff, refit, tuning split, and probability at full precision.
